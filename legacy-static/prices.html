Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer sessions in English?

Yes — every consultation is available in either English or Hungarian, whichever you're more comfortable in.

Are sessions online or in person?

Sessions take place online, which most clients find more comfortable, private, and easier to fit into daily life.

How long is the first session?

The first session runs 75–90 minutes, giving us enough time to get to know each other and understand what brought you here. Every session after that is 55 minutes.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

No problem, free of charge, up to 24 hours before your appointment. Cancelling within 24 hours adds 50% of the session fee to your next booking.

How do I know if the Self-Building Program is right for me?

Book a first conversation and we'll figure it out together. See the Self-Building Program page for a full breakdown of who it's for.

Is what I share with you confidential?

Yes. Every conversation is fully confidential, in accordance with professional ethical standards.
